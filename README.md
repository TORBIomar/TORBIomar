### 🚀 What I Do

- 📐 **3D WebCAD & Manufacturing** — In-browser parametric tube modeling, 2D flat-pattern unrolling, cutting-path calculation, and CNC G-code generation with **Three.js**, **React Three Fiber**, and **OpenCascade.js**.
- ⚙️ **Enterprise Backends** — Reliable RESTful architectures, authentication, and data pipelines with **Java** & **Spring Boot**.
- 🗄️ **Databases & Cloud** — Performant relational models and cloud workflows on **MySQL**, **Oracle DB**, and **OCI**.
- 🔍 **Vector Retrieval & AI** — Semantic search patterns with **ChromaDB** and the **Gemini API**.
